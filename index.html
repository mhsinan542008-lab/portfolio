    About

    I get close to small things and wait for them to look back.

    I'm Muhammed Sinan, a photographer drawn to the world at ground level. Most of these frames were made crouched in a garden: a lizard pausing on a stem, a jumping spider peering over a leaf, rain still sitting on an allamanda petal.

    I shoot in natural light and keep the edit honest, so the colour you see is the colour that was there. When the sky is clear, I point the lens up instead.
